One place for a real estate team’s buildings, leases, contacts, conversations and private comps. Value any U.S. commercial property in about a minute, with every comp and its source shown.

Problem

What a firm knows is scattered and slow to use

  • It walks out the door. Comps live in personal spreadsheets, lease dates in inboxes, contacts in phones. When someone leaves, the firm loses what they knew.
  • Valuations are slow. Pulling comps by hand takes hours, and the databases that speed it up are priced for big firms.
  • Numbers arrive without proof. A value with no evidence under it does not survive a client or lender who pushes back.
The solution

How it works

From an address to a client-ready report

  1. Value it. Enter an address. In about a minute: a value range, the comps behind it on a map, and a source on every comp, with your own and your firm’s comps blended in.
  2. File it. Add the property to the firm’s buildings. Its sheet gathers the firm’s reports, comps, leases and notes.
  3. Share it. Put the report on the firm’s shelf, push chosen comps from your Data, and discuss the deal in Messages.
  4. Track it. Upcoming lease deadlines sit on one list, and The Board flags new comps in the markets you follow.
  5. Deliver it. Send the client a PDF or PowerPoint with your firm’s logo, or a link you can revoke.
