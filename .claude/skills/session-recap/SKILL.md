# Session Recap

Summarize everything accomplished in the current session as plain-English bullets a non-programmer can read in under a minute.

## Rules

- Bullets only. Group under short bold headers when there are more than ~6 items (e.g. **Website**, **Email**, **Marketing**).
- Plain English — say what changed for the business, not the code. "Leads now get a follow-up email with their report link," not "refactored notifyByEmail into sendOutboundEmail."
- One bullet per accomplishment. Lead with the outcome. Keep each under ~20 words.
- Use real numbers where they exist (pages added, emails delivered, dollars spent). Never invent or estimate them.
- Close with two short sections when applicable:
  - **Still open** — anything pending or blocked, and who it's waiting on.
  - **Costs** — anything that cost money this session (purchases, API credits, subscriptions).
- Match length to the session: a small session gets a small recap. No padding, no filler bullets.
- If the session included failures or dead ends that matter (e.g. something tried and abandoned), include them honestly in one bullet each.
