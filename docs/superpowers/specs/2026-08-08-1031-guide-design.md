## Scope decision

v4's education item ships as **one public server-rendered guide page for
both audiences**, chosen by the owner over a broker-workspace-only piece and
over in-report cards. The reasoning: property owners find it while
researching a sale (SEO + the BOV funnel), and for brokers it IS the
practice tool — they hand the link to a client instead of re-explaining the
45/180-day mechanics. Content, not a calculator mandate: the one
interactive element is mechanical date arithmetic, never a tax computation.

## What the feature is

`GET /1031-exchange` — a single comprehensive guide:

- **What a 1031 exchange is**: defer capital-gains tax by reinvesting sale
  proceeds into like-kind real property; since the 2017 tax law (TCJA) it
  applies to **real property only**.
- **The workflow, in order** (numbered): know what the building is worth
  before listing (CTA into the valuation flow, labeled an automated
  estimate); engage a **qualified intermediary before closing** (proceeds
  the seller touches are taxable); closing starts the clock; **identify
  replacement properties in writing within 45 days**; **close on a
  replacement within 180 days**; report the exchange on the tax return
  (Form 8824).
- **Deadline dates widget**: enter a closing date, see the 45-day and
  180-day calendar dates. Client-side only — nothing is sent to any server,
  and the page says so. Stated assumptions directly under the result:
  calendar days, no extensions assumed, and the 180-day period can be cut
  short by the tax-return due date unless extended — talk to your tax
  advisor.
- **Identification rules** in plain English: 3-property, 200%, 95%.
- **Common pitfalls**: touching the proceeds; missing the written 45-day
  identification; boot (leftover cash or debt reduction is taxable);
  reverse and improvement exchanges exist and are specialist territory.
- **FAQ**: 6–8 Q&As, one array feeding both the visible accordions and the
  FAQPage JSON-LD (the `HOW_FAQ` pattern from `/how-it-works`).
- **CTA block**: value your building (into the app), and the BOV funnel
  line — CompNinja **connects you with a local broker**. One line for
  brokers pointing at `/brokers`.

## Compliance (non-negotiable, test-pinned)

- A visible education-not-advice box: this page is educational only, not
  tax, legal, or investment advice; CompNinja is **not** a brokerage, a
  qualified intermediary, or a tax advisor; consult qualified
  professionals before acting.
- Valuation CTAs say **automated estimate**, never appraisal.
- Broker copy is "connect you with a local broker", never that we are one.
- The widget computes **dates only** — no gains, no taxes, no dollar
  figures.
- Tests assert the presence of the compliance strings AND the absence of
  broker/QI/advisor self-claims.

## Architecture (approach A: own module, shared shell)

- **`guide-1031.js`** — a new pure module (no I/O, no requires, Node-only)
  owning all content:
  - `GUIDE_1031_FAQ` — the Q&A array (single source for accordions and
    JSON-LD);
  - `renderGuide1031Body()` — the page body HTML string, including the
    inline deadline-dates `<script>`;
  - `faqJsonLd(siteUrl)` — the FAQPage node for the `@graph`.
- **`server.js`** — a small route only: `GET /1031-exchange` renders
  through the existing `marketShell({ title, description, canonical, body,
  jsonLd })`, spreading `brandGraph()` plus the module's FAQPage node into
  one `@graph` (standing rule: reference `ORG_ID`, never inline a second
  Organization). The route matches on the **path only** (query strings must
  not 404). `sitemap.xml` gains the URL.
- **Links in**: the shared `MARKET_FOOTER` gains a "1031 exchange guide"
  item (one edit, covers every marketShell page); `/how-it-works`'s own
  footer gains the same; `/brokers` gains a one-line "send your clients our
  1031 guide" pointer. `index.html` is untouched (the account wall keeps
  crawlers off `/` anyway).
- Rejected: inline in server.js (the vault-page extraction exists because
  large pages inside server.js collide with server work — a live concern
  with a parallel session); a static HTML file (second copy of the chrome
  to drift).

**Page title** stays ≤ 60 chars ("1031 Exchange Basics | CompNinja") per
the SEO lesson in `docs/SEO.md` (the 68–82-char titles were truncated).

## Testing

- **`test/guide-1031.test.js`**:
  - the emitted inline script parses (`new Function`) AND executes against
    a DOM stub (the vault-page harness pattern): drive the closing-date
    input, assert the 45- and 180-day dates including a month/year
    rollover case and a leap-year case;
  - `faqJsonLd()` parses as valid FAQPage JSON-LD and its question count
    equals the visible accordion count in the body;
  - compliance strings present (not-advice box, "automated estimate",
    "connect you with a local broker"); negative assertions that no copy
    claims brokerage/QI/advisor status ("we are a broker" and kin never
    appear);
  - body contains no unescaped `${` leakage and no external `<script src>`
    dependencies (self-contained page).
- **`test/routes.test.js`** additions: `/1031-exchange` answers 200
  `text/html` on a bare server; a query string does not 404 it;
  `sitemap.xml` includes it.
- `npm test` after every task.
